Research Area

Quantum Field Theory

Quantum field theory underlies the group's work on nonequilibrium wave kinetics, curved-spacetime dynamics, effective actions and high-energy theory.

Key questions
  • — How do loop corrections reorganize kinetic descriptions?
  • — How do backgrounds modify field-theory observables?
  • — Which effective descriptions remain controlled far from equilibrium?
Methods
  • Perturbation theory
  • Diagrammatic methods
  • Kinetic theory
  • Quantum field theory in curved spacetime
