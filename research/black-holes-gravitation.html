Research Area

Black Holes & Gravitation

Black holes connect gravitation, quantum information and quantum field theory and provide a sharp testing ground for ideas about quantum spacetime.

Key questions
  • — What is the quantum structure of black holes?
  • — How does information emerge from gravitational systems?
  • — What role can primordial black holes play in early-universe physics?
Methods
  • General relativity
  • Semiclassical gravity
  • Quantum information
  • Cosmological perturbation theory
