No manifest T duality at order α′³

Steven Weilong Hsia, Ahmed Rakin Kamal, Linus Wulff

Physical Review D · 111 · L061904

INSPIRE recordarXiv:2411.15302 [hep-th]DOIPDF

Abstract

An analysis of field redefinitions and T-duality at order α′³ showing an obstruction to lifting the relevant lower-dimensional redefinitions to ten dimensions.
