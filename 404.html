Research

Questions that connect scales

Scientific areas linked directly to their people, projects and publications.

01
String Theory & Quantum Gravity

String dynamics, dualities, higher-derivative corrections and quantum aspects of gravitation.

02
Early-Universe Cosmology

Inflation, string cosmology, primordial black holes and high-energy dynamics of the early universe.

03
Quantum Field Theory

Quantum fields in equilibrium and nonequilibrium settings, curved backgrounds and effective theories.

04
Black Holes & Gravitation

Black-hole information, quantum black holes, gravitation and semiclassical spacetime physics.

05
String Compactifications

Calabi–Yau compactification, moduli stabilisation, flux backgrounds and lower-dimensional effective theories.

06
Mathematical Physics

Noncommutative geometry, representation theory, deformation quantization and geometric structures in physics.

Current Work

Featured projects

Current
Perturbative String Cosmology

A publication-linked research theme spanning perturbative moduli stabilisation, Kähler-modulus inflation and fibre-inflation scenarios.

Lead: Ahmed Rakin Kamal, Mishaal Hai, Sayeda Tashnuba Jahan and collaborators

Current
Higher-Derivative String Theory and Dualities

Higher-derivative corrections, M-theory/type-II reductions and duality constraints in string effective actions.

Lead: Ahmed Rakin Kamal and collaborators

Current
Time Dependent Backgrounds in String Theory and Early Universe Cosmology

Time-dependent internal geometry, flux backgrounds and cosmological solutions in higher-dimensional supergravity and early-universe theory.

Lead: Mahbubul Alam Majumdar (PI); Ratul Mahanta and Ahmed Rakin Kamal (Co-PIs)

Academic Programme

Jamal Nazrul Islam Memorial Winter School

The 2025 ICTP Physics Without Frontiers school at BRAC University's Residential Campus in Savar explored the mathematics of the universe through cosmology, string theory, black holes and mathematical physics.

The official programme lists Suvrat Raju, Michele Cicoli, Fernando Quevedo and Ashoke Sen among the invited experts, and identifies group members Mahbub Majumdar, Ahmed Rakin Kamal and Sayeda Tashnuba Jahan among the organisers.
