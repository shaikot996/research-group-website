Biography

Profile

I am a graduate student in theoretical physics at the University of Manitoba, where I work with Professor Andrew Frey and Professor Margaret Carrington. I completed my undergraduate degree in Physics at BRAC University in Bangladesh. My research interests are centered on theoretical high-energy physics, particularly string theory, string phenomenology, particle physics, and quantum information. During my undergraduate studies, I worked on warped compactifications in supergravity, time-dependent backgrounds in string theory, and early-universe cosmology.

Research

Current research

I am currently pursuing graduate research in theoretical physics at the University of Manitoba. My previous research has focused on warped compactification in supergravity, the role of negative-tension sources in compact internal spaces, and time-dependent backgrounds in string theory and early-universe cosmology.

Education

BSc in Physics, BRAC University, 2026. Undergraduate thesis: “Warped Compactification in Supergravity: A p-Brane Model.”

Research Experience

Undergraduate Research Assistant, BRAC University (December 2025 - May 2026). Worked on “Time Dependent Backgrounds in String Theory and Early Universe Cosmology.” Research Intern, ICTP Physics Without Frontiers: Physics for Bangladesh (July - October 2025). Studied time-dependent backgrounds in string theory, differential forms, variational methods, and nonlinear differential equations.
